Eye Tracking Project

Eye Tracking Project is a student-driven research project that explores the intersection of eye tracking, neural feedback, and emotional response to stimulative artworks. Using state-of-the-art gaze detection technology, the project aims to uncover how our minds react to art through subconscious responses.

Developed by Bohdan Hnat, 2025

How to use: Press "Begin Session" to begin calibration and eye tracking. Use "Reset Session" to clear current session data.
